AI Governance Toolkit · Discreet

Send personal data to an AI. Without sending it.

Discreet sits between an app and an AI model. It swaps NRICs, phone numbers, cards and other personal data for placeholders on the way out, puts them back on the way in, and records every request in a tamper-evident log.

Please don't type real personal data. Use the made-up examples. This demo runs on a mock model: no AI is called and nothing costs money. Discreet keeps only counts and keyed fingerprints of what you send, never the text.
